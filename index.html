How to read this

Medial axis. The set of centres of disks that touch the boundary in two or more places. It always exists for a closed region.

Construction. Voronoi diagram of a dense boundary sampling; interior Voronoi edges approximate the axis. Short-arc branches are pruned.

Trunk. The longest path through the pruned tree, smoothed by a cubic spline and optionally extended to the boundary.

Chord offset. Distance from the trunk to its perpendicular chord's midpoint. Shown as a diagnostic: it is zero where the two walls are locally parallel.

    Simple closed regions only. The medial axis is approximated from a boundary sampling at about 1 canvas unit spacing.
